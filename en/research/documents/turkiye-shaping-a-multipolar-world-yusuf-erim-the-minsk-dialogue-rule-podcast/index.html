Türkiye Shaping a Multipolar World

Alisiya Ivanova and Yusuf Erim · September 10, 2026

About this episode

In this episode, Alisiya Ivanova is joined by Yusuf Erim, Editor-at-Large and Türkiye analyst at TRT World. They talk about Ankara’s balanced foreign policy, managing threats from Israel and Iran, Türkiye’s role in NATO, how to approach Russia, and the projected stability of a multipolar world.

The Minsk Dialogue Rule

The Minsk Dialogue Rule is a podcast that brings together diverse perspectives on international security across the Euro-Atlantic and Eurasian space. It is based on a simple principle: two people who already agree with each other should not enter the same discussion room. Otherwise, another echo chamber is created.
