Viktoriya Zakrevskaya

Belarus–Ukraine

Viktoriya Zakrevskaya works as a media-consultant on crisis communication and CEO profiling (commercial and non-commercial organisations), she has implemented projects in Ukraine, UK, Belgium, USA, Israel, China, and Russia.

Viktoriya is also a PhD in Political Sciences candidate at Kyiv National University. She holds an MA in Social Sciences from Aarhus University (Denmark) and a BA in Political Sciences from the European Humanities University (Lithuania). She interned at the Eastern Europe Studies Centre (Lithuania) and worked at the Belarusian Institute for Strategic Studies in 2013-2016.

Her research interests include international relations, Ukraine’s domestic and foreign policy, EU’s foreign policy, Belarus’s foreign policy, and government regulation of the gambling industry.
