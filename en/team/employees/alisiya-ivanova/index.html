Alisiya Ivanova

Analyst, Communications Manager

Alisiya Ivanova is an analyst and a communications manager at the Minsk Dialogue Council on International Relations.

Alisiya has a Master's degree in International Relations from Central European University (Vienna, Austria). She is a graduate of the Belarusian State University with a Bachelor's degree in International Law.

Alisiya is a member of the Executive Committee of the Younger Generation Leaders Network on Euro-Atlantic Security (YGLN) and Co-Chair of its Diplomacy Working Group.

Alisiya is a 2026–2027 Arms Control Negotiation Academy (ACONA) Fellow. She is also a 2026 Future Leaders Program Fellow at the Hungarian Institute of International Affairs (Budapest, Hungary).

In 2019, Alisiya worked at the Belarusian Institute for Strategic Studies (BISS) and completed an internship at the Mission of the International Committee of the Red Cross in Belarus. Alisiya is a winner and participant of prestigious international law competitions (Jessup Moot-Court Competition (USA), Jean-Pictet Competition (Indonesia)).

Alisiya's research interests include international and regional security, military alliances, and arms control.
