Eastern Partnership Reflection Forum

December 12, 2017 · 27 photos

On 11 December 2017 Minsk hosted the Eastern Partnership (EaP) Reflection Forum, co-organised by the Austro-French Centre for Rapprochement in Europe, the Minsk Dialogue Track-II Initiative and Centre international de formation européenne. The event was held in cooperation with the Belarusian Presidency of the Central European Initiative and convened more than 50 international experts from 30 European countries, as well as a dozen representatives from national and European institutions.
