One Year of the Minsk-II Accords: Achievements, Challenges and Lessons Learned

February 14, 2016 · 31 photos

On 11 February 2016, one year after the signing of the Minsk-II accords, an international conference was held in Minsk. The conference gathered representatives of the Normandy group to discuss conflict resolution in Ukraine’s east. The experts analyzed the Minsk-II accords’ accomplishments and failures, as well as the challenges to the accords’ further implementation. Furthermore, concrete policy recommendations to all stakeholders in the eastern Ukraine conflict were produced.
