About this episode

Guarantees for Ukraine, Moscow’s new tactics, strikes against the Houthis, elections in Taiwan and Russia’s attack on NATO.

  • Treaty on Security Guarantees for Great Britain and Ukraine.
  • Changing Kremlin tactics in the Russian-Ukrainian war.
  • The tense situation in the Red Sea and the West's operation against the Yemeni Houthis.
  • Elections in Taiwan and prospects for the Taiwan-China conflict.
  • NATO preparations for a Russian attack on the eastern flank.

The episode is co-hosted by Dzianis Melyantsou and Alisiya Ivanova,
