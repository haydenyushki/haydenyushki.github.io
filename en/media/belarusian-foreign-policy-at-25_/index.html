Belarusian Foreign Policy at 25

May 24, 2017 · 42 photos

On 18-19 May 2017 Minsk hosted a conference timed to the series of 25-year anniversaries of the establishment of diplomatic relations with its key foreign policy partners. The two-day event was a good opportunity to analyse achievements and lost opportunities in the process of establishing and developing diplomatic relations with main foreign policy partners as well as to explore alternative approaches to conceptualising Belarus’s foreign policy.
