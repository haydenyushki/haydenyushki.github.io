Who is to blame for the opening of the Polish-Belarusian border?

Dzianis Melyantsou, Yauheni Preiherman and Alisiya Ivanova · September 24, 2025 · In Russian

Also on Mave · YouTube · Telegram · RSS

About this episode

In this episode, we discuss Polish-Belarusian relations, the US and Chinese influence in Eastern Europe, the Zapad 2025 military exercises, and Trump's speech at the UN.

  • Why did Warsaw decide to open the Polish-Belarusian border?
  • What political benefit did Poland gain from the escalation in relations with Belarus?
  • How did China contribute to the opening of the Polish-Belarusian border?
  • Zapad 2025: Key results of the Belarusian-Russian military exercises.
  • Donald Trump's speech at the General Assembly: Criticism of the UN and a change in rhetoric on the Russian-Ukrainian war.

From the Minsk Dialogue office — with Alisiya Ivanova, Denis Melyantsou, and Yauheni Preiherman.
