Yauheni Preiherman and Alisiya Ivanova · December 4, 2023 · In Russian

Also on Mave · YouTube · Telegram · RSS

About this episode

In this episode we are talking about the Berlin Forum, OSCE and NATO-Ukraine Council.

  • Berlin Foreign Policy Forum: sociological data from the “Berlin Pulse” and strategic priorities of Europe.
  • Diplomatic moves at a meeting of the OSCE Council of Foreign Ministers.
  • Results of the first meeting of the NATO-Ukraine Council.
  • “Putin appears to be winning the war in Ukraine” - an article by the Economist on the prospects for the Russian-Ukrainian conflict.

“The Berlin Pulse 2023/24” https://koerber-stiftung.de/site/assets/files/34911/the_berlin_pulse_20232024_1.pdf 

The episode is co-hosted by Alisiya Ivanova, and Yauheni Preiherman
