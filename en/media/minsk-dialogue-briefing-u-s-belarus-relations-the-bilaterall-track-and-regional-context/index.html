Minsk Dialogue Briefing «U.S.- Belarus Relations: the bilateral track and regional context»

November 7, 2018 · 17 photos

Among the speakers were Lt. Gen (Ret.) Frederick Benjamin Hodges, former Commander of the U.S. Army in Europe, and Michael Carpenter, former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense and Director for Russia at the US National Security Council.

The briefing was organised  by the Minsk Dialogue Track-II Initiative (Belarus), the Jamestown Foundation (USA), and the Konrad Adenauer Foundation (Germany).
