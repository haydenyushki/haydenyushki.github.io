Pan-European Integration Processes: Towards a Mutual Vision through Cross-Border Synergies

October 29, 2016 · 39 photos

On 27 October 2016 an international conference on pan-European integration processes took place in Minsk. The conference was organised by the Minsk Dialogue Track-II Initiative (Belarus) in cooperation with the Konrad Adenauer Foundation (Germany) and the Austro-French Centre for Rapprochement in Europe (Austria-France), and supported by the French Institute of International Relations (IFRI), and the French and Austrian Embassies in Belarus. The event brought together experts, academics and diplomats to discuss conceptual frameworks for advancing pan-European integration and to examine ways to advance pan-European integration processes by facilitating cross-border cooperation in various areas of mutual interest.
