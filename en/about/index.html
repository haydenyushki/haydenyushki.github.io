About

The Minsk Dialogue offers an open and geopolitically unbiased platform for research and discussion on international affairs and security in Eastern Europe.

History

It was launched as a Track-II initiative in early 2015. Its first international conference, The EU, Russia and the Shared Neighbourhood: Bridging the divide, took place in March 2015. On 26 April 2019 it was registered as the Minsk Dialogue Council on International Relations.

Regular Minsk Dialogue events bring together international experts, high-level officials and diplomats. The team and its expert network produce analytical reports, policy papers, commentaries, backgrounders and conference non-papers, which are widely shared with international stakeholders.

In May 2018 the inaugural Minsk Dialogue Forum gathered over 500 participants from 59 countries. Its title, Eastern Europe: In search of security for all, became the organisation’s motto.

Goals

  • To promote greater security in Eastern Europe.
  • To help Belarus advance its sovereign interests in the system of international relations.
  • To strengthen the Belarusian expert and academic communities in international relations and security.

Advisory and Expert Councils

The Advisory Council supports the organisation’s strategic development and its standing in international expert and diplomatic communities. Members of the Expert Council take an active part in its research agenda. Both councils include Belarusian and foreign academics, experts and diplomats.
