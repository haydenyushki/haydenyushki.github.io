Belarusian Foreign Policy at 25

Conference · May 18–19, 2017

Aims:

  1. To offer a historical perspective on the emergence and development of Belarus’s sovereign foreign policy and to contextualise its challenges and accomplishments.
  2. To analyse achievements and lost opportunities in the process of establishing and developing diplomatic relations with main foreign policy partners.
  3. To explore alternative approaches to conceptualising Belarus’s foreign policy.
