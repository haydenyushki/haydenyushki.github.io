Description

In the new episode of the "Last Monday" podcast, we discuss the parliamentary elections in Armenia, the dialogue between Paris and Minsk, Volodymyr Zelenskyy's open letter to Vladimir Putin, and the closed visit of a Russian businessman to Kyiv.

  • How can we explain the election results in Armenia: war, diaspora, and opposition?
  • How and why is the diaspora factor changing in Armenian political processes?
  • What can we expect in Armenia's relations with Belarus and Russia after Pashinyan's latest victory?
  • The dialogue between Paris and Minsk continues: what do these new details suggest?
  • Why did Zelenskyy write an open letter to Putin? And how does such an epistolary genre fit in with the state of affairs on the frontlines?
  • What does Roman Abramovich's visit to Kyiv indicate?

From the Minsk Dialogue office — with Dzianis Melyantsou and Yauheni Preiherman.
